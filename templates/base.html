Ancient Grains – The Heart of Civilization

For millennia, grains have sustained humanity—wheat, barley, rice, and maize built empires, fed armies, and shaped cultures. Our selection honors this legacy:

  • Einkorn & Emmer – The first domesticated wheat, unchanged since the dawn of farming.
  • Black Barley – A staple of gladiators and medieval peasants, rich in fiber.
  • Red Rice – Grown in Himalayan terraces for centuries, unpolished and nutrient-dense.

Each grain is sourced from heirloom seeds, milled traditionally. Order yours today — taste history.
